Privacy Policy

1. What we collect

DAT One stores the information you enter while using the app: user accounts, contact details (carriers, drivers, brokers), load information and uploaded documents. When you connect a Google account, we store the OAuth tokens needed to sync documents to Google Drive, load data to Google Sheets and send emails, plus the email address of the connected account.

2. How we use it

Your data is used exclusively to operate the app: manage loads and contacts, sync to your own Google Drive and Sheets, send new-user invite emails, and generate message drafts. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

3. Storage

Data is stored on our hosting provider's servers (Render.com) in a database. Uploaded documents are also copied to the connected user's own Google Drive. Access is protected by per-user accounts and passwords.

4. Your choices

You can disconnect Google at any time from the app's Settings page, delete loads, contacts and documents, and request account deletion from your administrator.

5. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
